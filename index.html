AetherClash

Lead a squad of painted chibi heroes into dark-fantasy tactical duels. Turn-based strategy for iPhone and iPad.

  • Six teams: Ashen Order and Crimson Host are free; four more teams are optional one-time purchases, with a bundle discount.
  • Single Player against an AI, Pass & Play on one device, and asynchronous online matches through Game Center.
  • No ads, no subscriptions, no tracking, and no data collected by the developer.
