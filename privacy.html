Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Summary

AetherClash does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. The developer has no servers for the game and receives no data about you.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask you to create an account, and it has no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting tools. Game settings and your match progress are stored only on your device.

Game Center

Online matches use Apple Game Center. When you play online, Apple handles sign-in, matchmaking and the transfer of match data between you and your opponent, and may show your Game Center alias and avatar to your opponent. This is governed by Apple's privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). The developer does not receive your Game Center identity or match data.

In-app purchases

Purchases of additional teams are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not see or store your payment details, name or Apple ID. The game only checks with the App Store which teams your Apple ID owns.

Advertising and tracking

AetherClash shows no ads and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Children

AetherClash does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
